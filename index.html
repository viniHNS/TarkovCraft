Tips & Tricks

    
    
      
  • Custom Item — Check "Custom" on any ingredient, tool, or output to paste a MongoDB ObjectId directly instead of searching the API.
    • 
      
  • MongoID — Click the MongoID button in the sidebar to generate and copy a fresh ObjectId to your clipboard instantly.
    • 
      
  • Production time is entered in minutes. A live preview (e.g. "= 1h 30m") appears as you type in the duration field.
    • 
      
  • Export — Use Copy All or Download .json to get your schema-compliant crafts.json for your SPT mod.
    • 
      
  • Item cache — Items from the Tarkov API are cached locally in your browser. Use Clear DB in the header to force a fresh fetch.
